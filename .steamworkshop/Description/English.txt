Archotech Android Hardware

[h1]About[/h1]
True archotechnology can't be built by human hands. But a salvaged vanometric power cell, psychic emanator, or violence generator can be dismantled, re-housed, and wired into an android chassis. This mod adds a family of such parts for androids from Vanilla Races Expanded - Android: alternative reactors with distinct power economies, plus archotech implants that lift limitations androids are normally born with.

Every part is crafted at the Android Part Workbench (Archotech Androidtech research) and installed via surgery. Implants can be removed and reactors swapped out, returning the intact item either way.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Reactors[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Vanometric reactor[/b] - a permanent power cell that removes the android's power need entirely. No more drain, no more replacements.
[*][b]Thanatic reactor[/b] - fed by the echoes of violent death. Each humanlike kill its host delivers recharges it, and brief overflows trigger a combat overcharge. Starved of kills it drains faster than a standard reactor, and its host dies when it runs dry. Requires the Anomaly DLC (built from a shard) or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2062943477]Vanilla Expanded - Power[/url] (salvaged from an archotech violence generator) to craft.
[*][b]Grav reactor[/b] - drawn from gravship engine technology and partially recharged (half a tank by default, configurable) whenever its host takes part in a gravship launch. Indispensable to a colony that lives on the move. Requires the Odyssey DLC to craft.
[/list]

[h2]Other Hardware[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Psychic transceiver[/b] - bridges an android's natural psychic inertness, granting psychic sensitivity.
[*][b]Archotech mnemocore[/b] - re-architects android memory onto self-organizing archotech substrate, removing the memory need entirely.
[*][b]Neutrosynthesizer[/b] - an android kidney that actively synthesizes fresh neutroamine, rapidly restoring neutro loss.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need Odyssey, Anomaly, or Vanilla Expanded - Power?[/b]
A: No. They only gate crafting recipes (Odyssey for the grav reactor; Anomaly or Vanilla Expanded - Power for the thanatic reactor). Everything else works without them, and parts obtained another way still function.

[b]Q: How do I get a reactor back out of an android?[/b]
A: Install a different reactor; the old one is ejected as an intact item with its charge preserved. Implants have their own removal surgeries. A thanatic reactor can also be extracted from its dead host's corpse.

[b]Q: My awakened android refuses reprogramming at the behaviorist station.[/b]
A: That is Vanilla Races Expanded - Android's normal behaviour. This mod adds two ways around it: a psychic transceiver implant, or the Self-Determination inspiration that awakened androids can roll.

[b]Q: Is the thanatic/grav reactor imbalanced?[/b]
A: Refill amounts, overcharge durations, and psychic sensitivity are all configurable in mod settings.

[b]Q: Was this made using LLMs!?[/b]
A: Some of the grunt code, and most localizations. I'm a software engineer by trade, and have been writing code for 25 years. All code is manually reviewed and tested; all bug reports and mod-conflicts followed up personally as time and detail allows. All art is either commissioned from humans, hand-authored in Krita, or both.
