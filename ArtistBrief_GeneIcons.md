`Gene_ThanaticReactor.png`
Androids with this hardware have an archotech reactor that draws power from the psychic echoes of violence. It is recharged when the android kills another humanlike. If it ever runs dry, it extracts a final charge from its host, killing them.

`Gene_GravReactor.png`
Androids with this hardware have an ultratech reactor that draws power from gravitational waves. It is recharged whenever the host participates in a gravship launch.

`Gene_ArchotechMnemocore.png`
Androids with this hardware have an archotech mnemocore that provides self-organizing, effectively infinite memory storage. Reformatting is unnecessary and memory never degrades.

`Gene_SuperNeutroSynthesis.png`
Androids with this hardware can actively synthesize non-trivial quantities of neutroamine, restoring lost internal supplies at a greatly accelerated rate.

`Gene_PersonaCore.png`
Androids with this hardware have had their subcore permanently transcribed onto superior archotech substrate. Cognition is sharpened beyond natural human capabilities.
